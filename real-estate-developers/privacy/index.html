Privacy Policy

Restate Deals · Panama City · Last updated: October 2026

Versión en españolceo@restate.deals

Who we are

Restate Deals (“we”) helps Panama real estate developers sell apartments to crypto and US buyers. We are based in Panama City. For anything about your data, write to ceo@restate.deals.

What we collect

  • What you send in our forms: name, company or building, WhatsApp number, email, apartment price range, number of units and the number of clients you need.
  • How you found us: the ad or link you came from (UTM tags, Facebook click ID), the page you landed on, the referring site, your browser type and IP address.
  • Cookies and similar storage: Meta Pixel cookies (_fbp, _fbc, up to 90 days), Google Analytics cookies (_ga, up to 2 years) and a small record of the first and last ad you came from, saved in your browser.
  • Statistics without cookies: Plausible Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics count visits and clicks without cookies and without identifying you.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request and send you a price or proposal, usually on WhatsApp.
  • To book and hold calls.
  • To measure our ads and the site: we tell Meta and Google Analytics that a form was sent or a button was pressed, so we can see which ads work and show fewer ads to people who are not interested.

The legal basis is your consent when you send a form, and our legitimate interest in measuring our own advertising.

Who we share it with

Meta PlatformsMeta Pixel and Conversions API (ad measurement). We send your phone, email and name only as irreversible SHA-256 codes, plus IP, browser and the ad click ID. WhatsApp, when you write to us.
GoogleGoogle Analytics 4 (visit and click statistics) and Google Workspace (Google Sheets), our CRM where we keep requests.
TelegramNotifications about new requests to our team.
CloudflareHosting, security and the server that receives the forms.
Plausible, CalendlyVisit statistics; call booking if you pick a time.

Some of these companies process data outside Panama, mainly in the United States and the European Union. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Requests: up to 24 months after our last contact, longer only if we work together or the law requires it (for example, anti-money-laundering rules). Meta cookies: up to 90 days. Google Analytics cookies: up to 2 years. The ad record in your browser stays until you clear your browser data.

Your rights

Under Panama Law 81 of 26 March 2019 on Personal Data Protection and its regulations, you can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or send it to you in a common format. You can also withdraw your consent at any time. Write to ceo@restate.deals from the email or WhatsApp number you gave us. We reply within the time set by law. You can also contact the national data authority (ANTAI).

To block ad cookies, use your browser settings, Meta’s ad preferences or the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works without them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page.
